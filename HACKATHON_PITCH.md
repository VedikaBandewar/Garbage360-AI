# Garbage360 AI — Hackathon Pitch

## One-line pitch

**Garbage360 AI converts citizen garbage reports into AI-powered waste intelligence — identifying the problem, prioritizing urgency, highlighting hotspots and helping verify cleanup.**

## Problem

Citizens can see garbage every day, but reporting does not automatically mean effective action.

Common problems:
- repeated/duplicate complaints
- lack of prioritization
- limited municipal resources
- poor visibility of persistent hotspots
- garbage-related drain blockage
- weak resolution verification

## Solution

Garbage360 AI creates a workflow:

```text
Report
 ↓
AI analysis
 ↓
Waste classification
 ↓
Severity + drain risk
 ↓
Priority
 ↓
Hotspot intelligence
 ↓
Cleanup
 ↓
Verification
```

## What makes it different?

The product does not stop at:

> "Complaint submitted."

It asks:

> "How serious is this, where is the hotspot, what should happen first, and was the problem actually resolved?"

## Core AI

- image/text understanding
- waste category
- severity
- drain-risk
- priority
- repeated-location signals
- optional multimodal vision model
- future image similarity and cleanup verification

## Example

Citizen uploads:

> Large mixed garbage pile beside a blocked drain.

AI:

```text
Category: Mixed Waste
Severity: Critical
Drain Risk: High
Priority: P1
Action: Immediate drain + garbage clearance
```

## Impact

### Citizens
- easier reporting

### Municipal teams

### Environment
